About Colin

Long Beach, California · BFA, Cal Poly San Luis Obispo

Hi there! My name is Colin. I'm from Long Beach, California, and hold a Bachelor of Fine Arts degree from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo.

While my professional journey has led me deeper into the world of technology, my passion has always remained rooted in the creative process — where brushes, pigments, and ideas spark something real.

I created Optic Art Supply for artists, designers, students, and creative professionals to explore new techniques, discover innovative tools, and find inspiration to enhance their unique style or message.
