Safco Facil Flat File
5-Drawer Steel · Small & Medium · Stackable
Colin Aug 2026 · Product Guide

The Safco Facil Flat File gives architects, engineers, designers, artists, and office teams a dedicated place for oversized documents. Drawings and other large sheets lie flat inside wide horizontal drawers instead of being folded, rolled, or stored upright in a standard filing cabinet.

Choose Between Small and Medium

Safco offers the Facil in two sizes. The Small 4969LG has 37-inch-wide drawers with 25.25 inches of depth. The Medium 4972LG steps up to 43-inch-wide drawers and 31.5 inches of depth — better suited for D- and E-size engineering drawings or large poster stock.

Five Drawers for Active and Archived Files

Each Safco Facil cabinet holds up to 300 sheets under active filing, 450 sheets for semi-active use, and 600 sheets for inactive storage. The different thresholds let the cabinet serve both working files and longer-term document archives in the same unit.

The Small model lists a 60-pound drawer capacity. For heavier collections, document weight and distribution should be considered alongside available drawer dimensions before loading.

Built for Regular Access to Large Files

Heavy-duty nylon rollers support drawer movement during all-day retrieval. Courtesy stops prevent drawers from rolling completely open. Chrome handles span the full drawer front for an easy grip without touching stored documents. Each drawer includes a label holder for project, client, year, or department identification.

The rear hood and hinged front depressor hold sheets flat inside each drawer and help limit edge curling during storage — a meaningful difference from cabinets that simply lay documents loose in an open tray.

Stack Safco Facil Flat Files for More Storage

Safco Facil units can be stacked up to two high when used with the matching closed base. A high base supports a single flat file and raises it to a more accessible working height. Small and Medium cabinets each use different base models.

Small Facil Base Options

  • 4970LG Closed Base — 40.25" W × 24" D × 4.75" H. For stacking two Small units.
  • 4971LG High Base — 40.25" W × 20.5" H. Raises one Small cabinet to working height.

Medium Facil Base Options

  • 4973LG Closed Base — 46.25" W × 30" D × 4.75" H. For stacking two Medium units.
  • 4974LG High Base — 46.25" W × 31.75" D × 20.75" H. Raises one Medium cabinet.
