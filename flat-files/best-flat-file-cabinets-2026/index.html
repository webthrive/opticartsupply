Best 5 Flat File Cabinets of 2026 Reviewed

Colin Sep 2026 - Buying Guide

A flat file cabinet is a large storage furniture type that uses wide, flat drawers to organize large-format items. The drawer sits flat rather than tilted or vertical. This reduces the risk of creases, wrinkles, or bending.

These cabinets are a standard tool in architectural firms, design studios, and art storage environments. They make it easy to store and retrieve maps, blueprints, drawings, prints, and large paper items without folding or rolling them.

Flat file cabinets come in different drawer sizes, materials, and configurations. Some are steel, some wood. Some have 3 drawers, while others have 7 or 8.

1. Bieffe BF Line Flat File - Best Overall

Price: $999 - $3,200+
Rating: 4.8/5
Best for: Professional studios, architectural firms, and large-format artwork storage

The Bieffe BF Line is the top pick because it handles large-format storage better than most of its rivals. It can store A0, A1, and A2-format documents, and the drawers are wide enough to support large architectural drawings without bending.

Most flat files only come in a 5-drawer version. The BF Line comes in 5 or 7 drawers. Users with a large volume of documents can opt for the 7-drawer version for more capacity without buying a second unit.

The cabinet sits on casters, so moving it around the studio is not difficult. It has a locking system that secures all drawers at once. A label slot on each drawer allows users to identify contents at a glance.

Important Features:

  • Steel construction
  • 5 or 7 drawer options
  • A0, A1, and A2 drawer sizes
  • Casters for mobility
  • Locking system for all drawers
  • Individual drawer label slots
  • Smooth drawer operation
  • Stackable design
  • Professional finish

Our Verdict:

The BF Line is the most complete flat file on this list. It covers size, drawer count, mobility, and security better than the competition. The price is higher, but for a professional studio, the investment is justified. The only limitation is drawer depth for a very large architectural drawing.

2. Hann Mobile Paper Storage Cart

Price: $300 - $800
Rating: 4.5/5
Best for: Classrooms, shared studios, artists who need to move storage frequently

Not every studio has a fixed layout. The Hann Mobile Paper Storage Cart is designed for environments where mobility is a priority. It runs on casters, so you can move it between workstations or store it out of the way when not in use.

The cart holds large paper sheets flat in open trays. This makes it easy to see what is stored in each tray without opening drawers. It works well in classrooms, open-plan studios, and shared workspaces where multiple people need access to the same storage.

Important Features:

  • Wheeled caster base
  • Open tray design
  • Multiple tray options
  • Compact footprint
  • Easy to reposition
  • Good for shared access
  • Lightweight construction

Our Verdict:

The Hann cart is the best choice when flexibility matters more than security. The open tray design means contents are visible but not enclosed. For a shared classroom or studio where people need quick access to paper stock, it works well. It is not the right choice if you need to lock or archive documents long-term.

3. Safco 5-Drawer Steel Flat Files

Price: $149.60 - $2,017.18
Rating: 5/5
Best for: Blueprints, plans, drawings, and high-volume professional storage

If you want a traditional flat file, the Safco 5-Drawer Steel Flat File is a strong option. It uses welded steel construction and a reinforced internal frame. Each drawer is about 2⅜ inches high, and ball-bearing side rollers make opening the drawer easy.

The drawer has a piano-hinged depressor in front. This gives more control when opening or closing the document drawer.

Important Features:

  • Welded steel construction
  • Five large flat drawers
  • Ball-bearing side rollers
  • Reinforced inner frame
  • Wrap-around cabinet corners
  • Stationary rear hook
  • Piano-hinged front depressor
  • Small, Medium and Large sizes
  • Large-format paper support
  • Professional office-friendly design

The small model drawer is about 37 x 26 inches, the medium is about 43 x 32 inches, and the large drawer is about 50 x 38 inches.

Our Verdict:

The strongest point of the Safco 5-drawer Steel File is its durability. For heavy-duty use, the steel body and the roller drawers are a good combination. One important fact: many providers make this item non-returnable because of its made-to-order configuration. So, when ordering, check the size and drawer requirements carefully.

4. Richeson Oak Flat Files

Price: $893.89 - $2,823
Rating: 4.2/5
Best for: Home studios, galleries and traditional interiors

Metal flat files look great in professional spaces, but they don't always fit a home, gallery, or traditional studio. The Richeson Oak Flat Files are different.

The Golden Oak stain and polished finish make the cabinet look like aesthetic storage furniture. The cabinet comes in 3-, 5-, and 8-drawer combinations. You can stack multiple units to create 10 to 15 drawers if needed.

Important Features:

  • Genuine wood-focused appearance
  • Golden oak stain
  • Smooth finished exterior
  • 3-drawer option
  • 5-drawer option
  • 8-drawer option
  • Stackable design
  • Large artwork storage
  • Home and professional studio-friendly
  • Furniture-style look

Our Verdict:

If design is important to you, Richeson Oak can be the most attractive option. It's a good fit if you want something more like furniture rather than an office cabinet. But the wooden build is not recommended for industrial use.

5. Safco Facil Flat Files

Price: $145.60 - $1,453.10
Rating: 4.9/5
Best for: Affordable professional flat storage

Not all buyers have the budget to spend $2,000 - $3,000 on a flat file. Safco Facil Flat File is comparatively affordable and gives you a proper steel cabinet within budget. Safco positions this line as an economical solution for non-archival storage.

The flat file cabinet can store art materials, engineering drawings, and large sets of documents. The steel drawers keep operations smoother and give the cabinet longevity.

Important Features:

  • Steel cabinet construction
  • Budget-friendly flat storage
  • Small and Medium sizes
  • Drawer label holders
  • Optional bases
  • High-base configuration
  • Closed-base configuration
  • Suitable for drawings and large documents
  • Professional design

Our Verdict:

If budget and durability are your preference, the Safco Facil can be a very balanced choice. But the manufacturer positions it as non-archival storage, so you'll need to consider preservation requirements if you want to store valuable archival materials long-term.

Final Words

The Bieffe BF Line Flat File is our Best Overall pick for 2026. Its steel construction, 5 or 7 drawers, A0/A1/A2 options, casters, and locking system cover what professional users need most of the time. If you want heavy-duty stationary storage, the Safco 5-Drawer Steel Flat File is a strong alternative. For the budget-focused buyer, Safco Facil Flat Files gives more value. If you desire a wooden cabinet, Richeson Oak Flat Files can be a good option. If you need mobility, the Hann Mobile Paper Storage Cart is more convenient.
