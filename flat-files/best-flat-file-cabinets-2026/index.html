Best 5 Flat File Cabinets of 2026 Reviewed

Colin Sep 2026 - Buying Guide

It's hard to store large drawings, artworks, blueprints, or pictures in a normal cabinet. Papers can wear out when folded, and it's hard to flatten them if you keep them rolled. This is where a flat file cabinet comes in. It helps keep large papers and artworks flat. But not all flat file cabinets are the same.

Some models are built with heavy-duty steel, while others are made of wood. Some are mobile, while others use an open tray design. In this article, we have compared the best 5 flat file storage options, their sizes, storage capacity, mobility, and build quality, and we help you decide which one is best for your needs.

1. Bieffe BF Line Flat File

Price: $1,932–$3,294.75
Rating: 4.8/5
Best for: Professional studios, architects, artists, and large-format document storage

The Bieffe BF Line is the most complete traditional flat file cabinet on our list. It combines durability, mobility, and drawer choice. The cabinet is made of sheet metal, and it has a scratch-resistant epoxy finish on the outside.

The casters let you move the storage from place to place as needed. You can get the cabinet in both 6- and 7-drawer configurations; it's also available in A2, A1, and A0 sizes.

Important Features:

  • Heavy-duty sheet-metal construction
  • Scratch-resistant epoxy finish
  • 5-drawer and 7-drawer option
  • A2, A1 and A0 document support
  • Built-in caster base
  • Metal drawer handles
  • Chrome label holders
  • Safety lock
  • Almost 2-inch-high drawers
  • Top and wheeled base included

A0 configuration drawers can hold up to 51 x 36 inches, while A1 drawers can hold up to 40.5 x 28.75 inches, and A2 drawers can hold up to 25.5 x 18.25 inches. The safety lock makes the cabinet useful in shared studios or commercial environments.

Our Verdict:

If you want to buy a premium flat file and use it long term, the Bieffe BF Line is the most balanced choice. This is a more professional storage system than a standard paper cabinet.

The main downside is the price. However, considering the build quality, locking system, and large-format options, the cost may be reasonable for professional users.

2. Hann Mobile Paper Storage Cart

The Hann Mobile Paper Storage Cart is slightly different than the traditional metal flat file. It works as both a storage cabinet and a mobile work surface.

The cabinet uses hard maple framing and birch paneling. The upper white Fiberesin surface is strong and even, so you can also use it as a work table. The biggest advantage is the mobility - to change the storage location, you won't need to empty the whole cabinet.

Important Features:

  • Five large storage drawers
  • Hard maple frame
  • Birch panel construction
  • Natural wood finish
  • White Fiberesin worktop
  • Mobile design
  • Large paper storage
  • Classroom-friendly height
  • Can be used as a work surface

Cabinet dimensions are approximately 48 inches wide, 35 inches high, and 28 inches deep. Each drawer is approximately 41 x 24 inches and 2¾ inches deep.

This drawer depth is somewhat greater than the traditional flat files. As a result, you can store art supplies along with papers.

Our Verdict:

Hann's design is very practical when you need to change storage in your studio or classroom. The upper work surface gives more value in a small space. However, it is not a direct replacement for the archival flat file. Compare the drawer dimensions before placing a very large architectural drawing.

3. Safco 5-Drawer Steel Flat Files

Price: $149.60–$2,017.18
Rating: 5/5
Best for: Blueprints, plans, drawings, and high-volume professional storage

If you want a traditional flat file, the Safco 5-Drawer Steel Flat File is a strong option. It uses welded steel construction and a reinforced internal frame. Each drawer is about 2⅛ inches high, and ball-bearing side rollers make opening the drawer easy.

The drawer has a piano-hinged depressor in front. This gives more control when opening or closing the document drawer.

Important Features:

  • Welded steel construction
  • Five large flat drawers
  • Ball-bearing side rollers
  • Reinforced inner frame
  • Wrap-around cabinet corners
  • Stationary rear hook
  • Piano-hinged front depressor
  • Small, Medium and Large sizes
  • Large-format paper support
  • Professional office-friendly design

The small model drawer is about 37 x 26 inches, the medium is about 43 x 32 inches, and the large drawer is about 50 x 38 inches.

Our Verdict:

The strongest point of the Safco 5-drawer Steel File is its durability. For heavy-duty use, the steel body and the roller drawers are a good combination. One important fact: many providers make this item non-returnable because of its made-to-order configuration. So, when ordering, check the size and drawer requirements carefully.

4. Richeson Oak Flat Files

Price: $893.89–$2,823
Rating: 4.2/5
Best for: Home studios, galleries and traditional interiors

Metal flat files look great in professional spaces, but they don't always fit a home, gallery, or traditional studio. The Richeson Oak Flat Files are different.

The Golden Oak stain and polished finish make the cabinet look like aesthetic storage furniture. The cabinet comes in 3-, 5-, and 8-drawer combinations. You can stack multiple units to create 10 to 15 drawers if needed.

Important Features:

  • Genuine wood-focused appearance
  • Golden oak stain
  • Smooth finished exterior
  • 3-drawer option
  • 5-drawer option
  • 8-drawer option
  • Stackable design
  • Large artwork storage
  • Home and professional studio-friendly
  • Furniture-style look

Our Verdict:

If design is important to you, Richeson Oak can be the most attractive option. It's a good fit if you want something more like furniture rather than an office cabinet. But the wooden build is not recommended for industrial use.

5. Safco Facil Flat Files

Price: $145.60–$1,453.10
Rating: 4.9/5
Best for: Affordable professional flat storage

Not all buyers have the budget to spend $2,000–$3,000 on a flat file. Safco Facil Flat File is comparatively affordable and gives you a proper steel cabinet within budget. Safco positions this line as an economical solution for non-archival storage.

The flat file cabinet can store art materials, engineering drawings, and large sets of documents. The steel drawers keep operations smoother and give the cabinet longevity.

Important Features:

  • Steel cabinet construction
  • Budget-friendly flat storage
  • Small and Medium sizes
  • Drawer label holders
  • Optional bases
  • High-base configuration
  • Closed-base configuration
  • Suitable for drawings and large documents
  • Professional design

Our Verdict:

If budget and durability are your preference, the Safco Facil can be a very balanced choice. But the manufacturer positions it as non-archival storage, so you'll need to consider preservation requirements if you want to store valuable archival materials long-term.

Final Words:

The Bieffe BF Line Flat File is our Best Overall pick. It will be our best overall pick for 2026. Its steel construction, 5 or 7 drawers, A0/A1/A2 options, casters, and locking system professional users need most of the time. If you want heavy-duty stationary storage, the Safco 5-Drawer Steel Flat File is a strong alternative. For the budget-focused buyer, Safco Facil Flat Files gives more value. If you desire Wooden cabinet, Richeson Oak Flat Files can be a good option. If you need mobility, the Hann Mobile Paper Storage Cart is more convenient.
